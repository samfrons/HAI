# About FEWS NET

## What FEWS NET is

FEWS NET (the Famine Early Warning Systems Network) is a leading provider of early warning and analysis on acute food insecurity around the world. It operates through partnerships to monitor and report food security conditions in dozens of countries, delivering timely, accurate, evidence-based information and analysis intended to inform international, national and local decision-making.

## History and leadership

The United States Agency for International Development (USAID) created FEWS NET in 1985, in response to the severe famines in East and West Africa earlier that decade and a recognised need for better, earlier warning of potential food security crises. USAID continues to create and oversee FEWS NET. The network functions through a partnership model involving US government agencies (including NASA, NOAA, USDA and USGS) alongside academic institutions and private implementing firms such as Chemonics International, the American Institutes for Research, and Tetra Tech. The FEWS NET website itself notes that the information it provides is not official U.S. Government information and does not represent the views or positions of the U.S. Department of State or the U.S. Government.

## What FEWS NET does

- **Monitoring and analysis.** FEWS NET continuously tracks existing and emerging threats to food security globally, and maintains the capacity to respond to unexpected crises anywhere in the world, sustaining operational networks in regions experiencing or at risk of acute food insecurity.
- **Data collection and sharing.** FEWS NET captures and distributes food-security information through its website and the FEWS NET Data Explorer, making it accessible to partners and the public.
- **Root cause analysis.** FEWS NET investigates the underlying drivers of persistent food insecurity and malnutrition, examining how local vulnerabilities interact with environmental hazards to produce crises.
- **Agroclimatology.** FEWS NET's technical teams conduct remote sensing, field observation and monitoring to generate early-warning products, drawing on multiple partners for data collection support.
- **Methodology.** FEWS NET's core method is scenario development: roughly every four months, FEWS NET analysts use a consistent eight-step process to assess the current food security situation in an area of concern, build assumptions about the future, and project how those assumptions will affect the food and income sources of poor households. Based on the convergence of evidence produced this way, analysts determine the most likely scenario and classify expected food insecurity outcomes using the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC). FEWS NET also applies a livelihoods approach — examining how households cope with a shock such as drought, flood, market disruption or conflict — and continues to monitor complex drivers including weather and climate, conflict, agricultural production, markets and trade, and nutrition.
- **Partnerships.** FEWS NET treats collaboration as core to its model, on the view that working with partners produces more insightful information than working alone, and the network aims to strengthen country-led food security efforts through evidence-based risk management and policy development.
