## 📊 Technical Specifications

### Model Configuration
- **Base**: meta-llama/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct
- **Method**: LoRA (r=16, alpha=32)
- **Quantization**: 4-bit (bitsandbytes)
- **Context**: 2048 tokens
- **Training**: 3 epochs, ~5-10 hours

### Hardware Requirements
- **Training**: 12-16GB VRAM (or 8GB with optimizations)
- **Inference**: 4-8GB VRAM (or CPU)

### Data Statistics
- **Base samples**: 10 (expert humanitarian Q&A)
- **Synthetic**: 200 (question templates)
- **Target**: 500-2000 (with enhancements)
